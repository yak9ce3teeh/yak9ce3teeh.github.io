Drafts and recovery

Starting the local companion
  1. Double-click Paper Reader.cmd in your Papers folder to open the private local workspace.
  2. Ensure Zotero 10 is open; use Authorize Zotero to grant access when prompted.
  3. The launcher connects automatically. For a public-page connection, copy the temporary session link from the local workspace and use its token here only if your browser allows localhost access.
  4. Translation needs a configured DeepSeek key on the companion; keys are never requested by this page.
